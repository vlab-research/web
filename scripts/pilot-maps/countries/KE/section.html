What does it cost to recruit survey respondents in Kenya?

One Virtual Lab study, from 16 July to 24 September 2026, recruited 3,636 qualified respondents aged 18 to 30 across Kenya for $21,649 in advertising, or $5.95 each. Here a qualified respondent is someone who finished the whole survey. A man cost $5.48 and a woman $6.65.

How it works · what the numbers count

Cost per qualified respondent

Every study sets quotas: how many people it needs from each group, such as women aged 30–44 in one province. A qualified respondent is someone who fell into one of those groups and completed the screening questions. People who started the survey but did not qualify are not counted, but the advertising that reached them is. So a study looking for a narrow group pays more per qualified respondent.

Advertising cost is what Meta reported spending on the study's ads, in US dollars, divided by qualified respondents. It excludes the incentive paid to respondents, the survey platform and our fee.

Women cost more because fewer finished

The study advertised separately to men and to women aged 18 to 30, anywhere in Kenya. The ads brought slightly more women into the survey than men, 3,452 against 3,376, for less money. But 43% of the women reached the last question, against 64% of the men. So each woman who finished cost $6.65 in advertising, and each man $5.48.

Qualifying meant finishing the survey

A study can count a respondent after a few screening questions, or only at the end. This one counted a respondent only when they answered the last question of a survey on young people's health, a median of 16 minutes after they began for men and 19 minutes for women. Everyone who stopped earlier was paid for in advertising and not counted, which is why the cost per qualified respondent is higher than a short screener would give.

How the study was set up

Quotas
Gender (men, women), ages 18–30, the whole country: 2 groups. The quotas began weighted towards women and were equal from 17 August.
Channel
Facebook and Instagram ads leading to a survey in Messenger, in English or Swahili. Incentive: prepaid mobile credit, not included in the advertising cost. People who had answered an earlier round were excluded from the ads.
Qualified
Arrived through one of the two groups' ads, and answered the survey's last question. 3,636 of the 6,900 who started did.
