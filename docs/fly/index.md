title: "Fly Surveys"
---

**Fly** is a chatbot survey tool, optimized for longitudinal surveys and incentive
reimbursement. A questionnaire runs as a conversation inside a messaging app —
Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp — rather than as a web form.

Start with [Core Concepts](/docs/fly/core-concepts/) — surveys, forms, and how forms
stitch together.
